## whoami

About Me

My passion started with a simple question: how do systems actually get breached? That curiosity grew into a focus on reverse engineering and malware analysis — dissecting malicious samples byte by byte to understand exactly how they work, then documenting it all in precise technical reports.

Alongside that, I work in Incident Response — collecting and analyzing digital evidence, containing breaches, and getting systems back to a secure state as fast as possible.

I believe the best defense starts with a deep understanding of the attack, which is why I build my own tools for system hardening and automated security scanning instead of relying only on off-the-shelf solutions.
